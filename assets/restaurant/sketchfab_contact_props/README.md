# Restaurant props

These USD conversions retain the visual meshes used by the final restaurant layer stack.

- **Wooden Dirty Crates – Broken Whole 2 Piece Set**, by Sunbox Games. The model was converted from glTF to USD with local texture references. [Original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/wooden-dirty-crates-broken-whole-2-piece-set-1ebaf13ac44748baa845ab7f5cb3fc9f) · [CC BY 4.0 attribution](crates/source/license.txt)
- **Simple Trash Can**, by Andrew.Mischenko. The glTF was converted to USD and scaled in the authored scene. [Original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/simple-trash-can-beb82ad22e354b1e900b7756b55c9ac0) · [CC BY 4.0 attribution](simple_trash_can/source/license.txt)
